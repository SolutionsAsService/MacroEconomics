# Critic: Inclusion (Holmes), R0

**Anchor used:** Kat Holmes, Mismatch: exclusion is a mismatch between a person and the interface, not a trait of the person. Solve for one, extend to many; design along the persona spectrum (permanent, temporary, situational).

**Observed:**
- The inherited repo has no page and no README, so every non-developer is excluded outright. The mismatch is total.
- Source files are invalid JSON and mix units ("304,000 million barrels" vs bare numbers vs unknown "Production_2021"). A newcomer, or a reader on a small phone or slow data, cannot tell what a number means.
- The CFA franc file lists "Facilitates exports and imports" under Criticism. The people it concerns (residents of the 14 member countries, many French-speaking) get a one-sided account of their own currency, with no French and no voice from them.
- AllocationStrategies.yaml promises "steadier returns" with no risk disclaimer. A person considering crypto, possibly in financial stress (situational), is mismatched by a persuasive but unsafe frame.
- QuadraticFunding.yaml has a wrong formula. A student who trusts it learns an error.
- Oil reserves disagree across EIA, OPEC and BP, and the repo hides that. Journalists cannot cite what has no source.
- Inflation rows mix countries and aggregates and skip years, so silent gaps read as zeros.

**Verdict:** OBJECT

**Requested change (concrete):**
1. Every chart gets a text table alternative, a plain-language one-line takeaway, a units label, and keyboard and screen-reader access. Colour is never the only encoding.
2. Each page opens with "what this is, in plain words", a glossary link, and no jargon undefined.
3. Offer French on the CFA franc page at minimum; show gaps as gaps and name the criticisms with sources.
4. Show disagreeing sources side by side, never one blended number. Every dataset page carries a source, date and caveat.
5. The strategies page carries a prominent risk statement. The calculator uses the correct formula, with a worked example.
6. Light weight pages, usable on a phone offline of heavy scripts, reduced-motion respected, and per-page data downloads for reuse.

**Statement of meaning:** A site about money and power fails when the people most affected by it cannot read it, check it, or see themselves in it.
