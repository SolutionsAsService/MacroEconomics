# Critic: Formalist (Arnheim) - R0

**Anchor used:** Arnheim, Art and Visual Perception: balance (visual weight by size, position, isolation), simplest structure, and the tension a cut-off shape produces.

**Observed:** No page exists; only the data can be judged. The one complete, single-snapshot series is OilProductionData1.json: Sep 2023, about 15 producers, barrels per day, OPEC flag (US 12.9M, Russia 9.48M, Saudi 9.06M...). Inflation is invalid JSON, 18 alphabetical rows with year gaps, countries mixed with aggregates: a line chart would fake continuity and ragged ends. Reserves disagree across three sources and units. CPPI has too many ranked items to read as one gestalt. Gaps and long lists give ragged, cut-off silhouettes, the failure the commissioner named.

**Verdict:** OBJECT to a line chart, map or multi-series hero; the oil snapshot is acceptable as the base.

**Requested change:** One horizontal bar chart, top 10 producers, as a single stepped wedge, longest bar on top, all left-anchored to one axis. Scale: full content width and 60-65% of first-screen height at 1440x900; at 390 px wide the same bars, 18-22 px thick, labels above or inside, no horizontal scroll, no clipping. OPEC members one saturated colour, others neutral grey: one contrast, so the eye lands on the Gulf cluster. Heaviest bar at top-left, the strongest position. Title above at secondary weight, one-line caption below ("September 2023, barrels per day"). No gradients, no second chart, no competing image. Inline SVG with a viewBox so it is complete at every width.

**Statement of meaning:** A few countries carry most of the weight, and the picture says it before any word does.
