Inflation

What does 100 from 1960 buy today?

In plain words: inflation is how fast prices rise. Instead of a line of yearly percentages, this page follows 100 units of money from the start of 1960 and shows how much of 1960's shopping it would still buy each year.

Every row in the file

Each bar is one year's inflation, 1960 to 2022, on the same scale. Bars stop at 50%; anything higher is drawn red and its peak named. A dot on the line is a year with no figure inside the row's range; nothing is drawn where the series has not started. Regions are marked: they are averages, not countries.
