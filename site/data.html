About the data

Where every number comes from, and what was fixed

In plain words: this site began as a folder of data files, three of which could not be opened by a program. They were repaired, not replaced: the originals are kept unchanged, and every repair is listed below. The wider-economy pages add open datasets from the World Bank, US government agencies, NOAA, the Federal Reserve and others, downloaded unchanged into _src/raw/macro/ and cleaned by _src/clean_macro.py, which never fills a gap.

Oil

US EIA, OPEC and BP proved reserves for 41 countries (million barrels); production for 10 countries, September 2023 (barrels per day).

Download oil.json

Repairs

  • Mexico's entry is cut off mid-line in the inherited file; only its US EIA figure (5,800 million barrels) survives
  • fields labelled '(bbl)' in the second half of the 'Countries' block hold the same values as 'million barrels' (Nigeria 37,000 = 37 billion barrels) and are read as millions of barrels
  • the 'OilData' block's 'Other_Reserves' (e.g. Oman 980000) and 'Production_2021' (e.g. Oman 15) have no stated unit and do not match any of the other figures; both are dropped
  • 'Years of Production in Reserve' is not copied; it is recomputed from US EIA reserves and 2021 production where both exist
  • OPEC membership added (twelve members as of 2024; Angola left in January 2024): the inherited reserves file marked only Gabon and Congo, and the production file flags members only for its ten countries

Inflation

World Bank consumer price inflation, annual %, 18 rows (Aruba to Bangladesh), 1960 to 2022.

Download inflation.json

Repairs

  • removed 2 comment line(s) of the form '// ... continue with null for years without data ...' and added 1 missing comma(s) between rows
  • 18 rows: the World Bank file's rows from Aruba to Bangladesh only; the rest of the alphabet is not in the inherited file
  • HistoricInflationDataSchema.json is empty in the inherited repo; nothing to carry over

Ports

Container Port Performance Index 2021, 287 ports, two ranks each.

Download ports.json

Repairs

  • removed 13 exact duplicate rows; 287 ports remain, of an index that ranks 370: the inherited file is partial
  • 12 ports have no statistical-approach rank in the inherited file; they are shown with the administrative rank only

CFA franc

History, institutions, members, rate history and reform of the CFA franc.

Download cfa_franc.json

Repairs

  • moved 'Facilitates exports and imports between France and member countries' from Criticism to a new ClaimedBenefits list: it states an advantage, not a criticism

Funding and crypto strategies

Nine funding mechanisms, quadratic funding, and eight crypto allocation strategies, as written in the inherited files.

Download funding.json

Repairs

  • QuadraticFunding.yaml stated the formula as 'The final amount received by a project = Direct contributions + (Square of the sum of the square roots of individual contributions) * Matching pool', which multiplies by the whole matching pool; replaced with the standard rule (Buterin, Hitzig and Weyl, 'A Flexible Design for Funding Public Goods', 2019)

Countries: GDP, people, prices, carbon

216 countries and 49 regions and groups: GDP in current US dollars (World Bank, 1960 to 2023), population (1960 to 2025), consumer price inflation and CPI index (World Bank), and CO2, CO2 per person and energy per person (Our World in Data, 1950 to 2024). Licences: CC-BY-4.0 and PDDL.

Download macro_countries.json

Download the same country data as one CSV, a row per country and year

Repairs

  • World Bank rows that are not countries (regions, income groups, the world: 49 codes) are kept apart from the 216 countries, so no ranking mixes them; the test is whether the code is an ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 country code (Kosovo, XKX, is added by hand: it has no ISO code but is a World Bank economy)
  • the country's region comes from the ISO country-codes file (UN M49 regions), not from the World Bank, so 'Europe' here follows the UN, not the World Bank's 'Europe & Central Asia'
  • GDP is in current US dollars (not adjusted for prices), so growth in it mixes real growth, inflation and exchange-rate moves; the pages say so wherever it is compared across years
  • the World Bank CPI index series is kept in the data file but not charted: its base year is not stated and some countries' values are not comparable between 2000 and 2023 (Albania's index rises 95 times, others fall to zero or below), which looks like changes of base or currency rather than prices
  • CO2 per capita, CO2 and energy per capita are taken from Our World in Data's CO2 file (columns trimmed, years from 1950 only); its population and GDP columns are not used

Markets and US data

Monthly Brent and WTI oil (US EIA), gold since 1833 (World Bank Pink Sheet, National Mining Association), the S&P 500 and P/E10 since 1871 (Robert Shiller, FRED), Henry Hub natural gas (US EIA), US CPI since 1913 and US employment since 1941 (BLS), global fossil CO2 since 1750 (Global Carbon Project) and Mauna Loa CO2 (NOAA); exchange rates for 22 currencies (Federal Reserve), as annual means. Licence: PDDL.

Download macro_markets.json

Repairs

  • S&P 500: the file's PE10 column holds 0.0 for 1871 to 1880 (a ten-year average does not exist yet) and again from October 2023, where Shiller's earnings data ends; the dividend, earnings and real-price columns also go to 0.0 from mid-2023. The 156 months with PE10 = 0 are treated as missing, not as a ratio of zero, and only the index level is used to the latest month
  • US employment: figures are thousands of people aged 16 and over (the BLS table 'cpsaat01'); the file's footnotes column marks years in which the survey was redesigned, and those breaks are not smoothed

Exchange rates

Annual average exchange rates, local currency units per US dollar, for 22 currencies, from the Federal Reserve's daily H.10 and G.5 series via the Open Knowledge Foundation. Included in the markets file.

Repairs

  • Exchange rates: the file's readme says Australia, the euro, Ireland, New Zealand and the United Kingdom are quoted as US dollars per local unit, but the values (Australia 0.90 in 1971, 1.42 in 2026) show they are local units per US dollar like all the rest; all 22 are used as local units per US dollar
  • Venezuela's series is kept in the data but left out of the currency charts: the bolívar was redenominated (2008, 2018, 2021) and the file does not say which unit each year is in
  • Exchange rates are annual averages of the daily rates, where the Federal Reserve published one that day; a year with few days (the first and the current year) is a part-year average and is marked so on the page

Climate series

Global fossil CO2 (Global Carbon Project), atmospheric CO2 at Mauna Loa (NOAA) and country CO2 (Our World in Data). Included in the markets and countries files.

Repairs

  • CO2 parts per million (Mauna Loa): the inherited file's rows carry more fields than its header names, so only the third column, 'Average', is used (March 1958 = 315.71, matching NOAA); the interpolated and trend columns are dropped because they cannot be told apart in the file
  • Global fossil CO2: million tonnes of carbon (not of CO2; multiply by 3.664 for CO2), as in the Global Carbon Project's file; per-capita is tonnes of carbon per person

Price prediction

A script, not data.

Repairs

  • PricePrediction.py fits a straight line through prices from historical_prices.csv, a file not in the repo; it is kept in _src/raw/ and not published as a forecast

The originals

The inherited files are kept unchanged in _src/raw/ in the source repository, and the repository as it stood before this redesign is on the branch legacy-2026-10-30. The repairs are made by _src/clean.py; anyone can rerun them.
