Crypto strategies

Ways people invest in crypto, and how each one loses

In plain words: these are common plans for buying and holding cryptocurrency. Each is set beside the way it goes wrong, at the same weight.

This is not investment advice. Cryptocurrencies can lose most or all of their value. Nothing here is a forecast or a recommendation, and no returns are shown because the inherited data holds none.

Dollar Cost Averaging

Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the asset's price.

The case made for it

  • Smoother entry into the market.
  • Lower average purchase price over time.

How it loses

  • Missed opportunities during rapidly rising markets.

Value Averaging

Adjusting the amount invested to maintain a target growth path for the portfolio.

The case made for it

  • Potentially higher returns by enforcing discipline in adding more during dips.

How it loses

  • Requires more capital to adjust the investment during downturns.

Buy And Hold

Purchasing cryptocurrency and holding it long-term, regardless of market fluctuations.

The case made for it

  • Lower transaction costs.
  • Compounded growth over the long term.

How it loses

  • Exposure to prolonged bear markets.
  • Missing short-term profits from market fluctuations.

Balanced Portfolio

Diversifying investments across a range of cryptocurrencies to spread risk.

The case made for it

  • Mitigates the impact of volatility on the portfolio.
  • Steadier returns over time.

How it loses

  • Diluted gains from high performers by balancing with lower performers.

Growth Focused

Allocating a larger portion of the portfolio to high-risk, high-reward cryptocurrencies.

The case made for it

  • Potential for substantial gains.

How it loses

  • Greater exposure to volatility and potential for significant losses.

Income Generating

Focusing on cryptocurrencies that offer dividends or interest through staking or other means.

The case made for it

  • Provides a steady income stream in addition to potential capital gains.

How it loses

  • Dependency on the specific mechanisms and reliability of dividend or interest payments.

Sector Rotation

Rotating investments among different crypto sectors (DeFi, NFTs, Layer 1 protocols) based on market cycle phases.

The case made for it

  • Opportunity to maximize returns by being in the right sector at the right time.

How it loses

  • Timing the market incorrectly and missing out on gains.

Hedging

Using derivatives or other cryptocurrencies to offset potential losses in the portfolio.

The case made for it

  • Reduced portfolio volatility.
  • Protection against downside risk.

How it loses

  • Cost of hedging can reduce overall profits.
  • Complexity and risk of derivatives if used as a hedge.
