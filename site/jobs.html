Jobs

Work in the United States since 1941

In plain words: The unemployment rate is the share of people who want a job and are looking but have none. The participation rate is the share of everyone aged 16 and over who is working or looking. A fall in participation can hide unemployment, so the two belong together.

US BLS via the Open Knowledge Foundation's datasets/employment-us (PDDL). Only the United States is covered. About the data
