Prices

Where prices are rising, and how fast

In plain words: Inflation is the yearly rise in what a typical basket of goods costs. A rate of 2% is gentle; at 50% a year, prices double in under two years. The data here is consumer price inflation as the World Bank collects it.

World Bank via datasets/inflation, and BLS via datasets/cpi-us (PDDL). The older inflation page follows 100 units of money from 1960. About the data
